SLIDE 2: Problem Statement
TITLE:
Clinical Problem & The Supervised Annotation Bottleneck

CORE MESSAGE:
Gliomas infiltrate irregularly across 3D white matter; manual 3D annotation is severely constrained, causing
supervised convolutional networks to suffer catastrophic background collapse in low-data regimes.

DEEP DIVE & THE NEUROIMAGING GAP:
* Biophysical Nature of Gliomas:
  - Gliomas infiltrate diffusely along 3D axonal tracts without clear anatomical borders.
  - Slice-by-slice 2D models discard through-plane volumetric continuity and corrupt 3D distance metrics (d_HD95).
* The Annotation Bottleneck:
  - Delineating 3D necrotic core, infiltrative edema, and active angiogenic margins requires hours of expert
    neuroradiologist time per patient.
* Supervised Collapse Under Label Scarcity:
  - On 100% labels (1,144 volumes), 3D Residual UNet achieves 62.54% mean Dice (80.96% WT).
  - When training scans drop to 1% (12 volumes), 3D Residual UNet completely collapses to 4.29% mean Dice
    (11.85% WT), and 3D nnU-Net drops to 20.69%. Supervised inductive bias alone cannot prevent collapse.
* Why Standard Computer Vision SSL Fails on Brain MRI:
  - The Contrastive False-Negative Penalty (SimCLR, MoCo): Contrastive learning treats all other batch patients
    as negative pairs. But all human brains share identical macroscopic anatomy (bilateral hemispheres, ventricles).
    Penalizing other patients penalizes healthy brain structure, degrading feature representations.
  - The Momentum Teacher Flaw (I-JEPA, DINO, BYOL): Standard JEPA relies on an Exponential Moving Average (EMA)
    teacher. EMA is an empirical heuristic with no convergence proof, requires brittle schedules, and consumes
    ~40% extra GPU memory—which is prohibitive for volumetric 3D grids.

SPEAKER SCRIPT:
"Gliomas are fundamentally three-dimensional, infiltrative tumors. Segmenting their necrotic core, edema, and
enhancing rims requires hours of expert neuroradiologist time. While supervised models like nnU-Net perform well
with hundreds of annotated scans, they fail dramatically when labels are scarce: at 1% labels (just 12 patients),
standard 3D Residual UNet completely collapses to 4.3% Dice, and nnU-Net falls to 20.7%.
Why couldn't we simply apply standard SSL? First, contrastive methods penalize other patients as negative pairs—which
penalizes shared macroscopic brain anatomy. Second, standard JEPA uses an EMA teacher network to prevent collapse,
which is an empirical heuristic with no convergence proof and adds 40% memory overhead. We needed a teacher-free
single-encoder framework with mathematically grounded anti-collapse regularization."

  4. t2f (T2-FLAIR): Suppresses free CSF fluid to reveal non-enhancing infiltrative tumor and vasogenic edema (SNFH).
* The Hierarchical Clinical Target Sub-Regions:
  - Whole Tumor (WT): Labels {1, 2, 3} (Total abnormal pathological volume).
  - Tumor Core (TC): Labels {1, 3} (Surgically resectable mass: necrosis + non-enhancing core + enhancing tumor).
  - Enhancing Tumor (ET): Label {3} (Active angiogenic, viable neoplastic rim).
  - Resection Cavity (RC, label 4): Post-surgical void, explicitly excluded from active tumor metrics per official protocol.

  - Zero-Leakage Enforcement: P_train ∩ P_val = ∅ and P_train ∩ P_test = ∅, grouped strictly by base patient identifier.

* Fractional Annotation Tiers (Nested Training Subsets):
  - 1% (12 vols), 5% (58 vols), 10% (115 vols), 25% (286 vols), 50% (572 vols), 100% (1,144 vols).

SLIDE 4: Methodology: 3D JEPA Architecture & Brain-Aware Masking
TITLE:
Methodology: 3D Latent JEPA Pipeline & Brain-Aware Target Masking

CORE MESSAGE:
Latent space prediction avoids reconstructing high-frequency scanner noise; zero-leakage context gathering
slashes attention FLOPs by 85.9% while brain-aware masking blocks interpolation shortcuts.

DEEP DIVE:
* Offline Preprocessing:
  1. Non-Zero Bounding Box Crop: Removes ~60% uninformative empty background air.
  2. Canonical Resampling: Resamples bounding box to an isotropic 128 x 128 x 128 grid (1.0 mm^3 spacing).
  3. Parenchyma-Only Z-Score: Normalizes intensity strictly on non-zero brain tissue (X - mu_nz) / sigma_nz.
* Tokenization & Embeddings:
  - 8^3 voxel non-overlapping Conv3d patch embedding -> 16^3 = 4,096 tokens (D = 384).
  - Fixed 3D sinusoidal positional embeddings encode spatial coordinates across depth, height, and width.
* Brain-Aware Anisotropic Target Masking:
  - 6 Anisotropic Cuboids: Sampled across axial, coronal, and sagittal slabs to match non-axis-aligned glioma morphology (776 tokens).
  - Parenchyma Constraint: Rejects candidate cuboids with <25% brain parenchyma (never wastes gradients predicting empty air).
* Connected 3D BFS Context Gathering:
  - Context tokens (1,536 tokens, 37.5% volume) are grown via 3D Breadth-First Search into a single contiguous cluster.
  - Zero-Leakage Self-Attention: Only visible context tokens enter the 8-layer ViT encoder:
    Attention FLOP Ratio = (1,536 / 4,096)^2 = (0.375)^2 = 0.1406 ===> 85.9% Attention FLOP Reduction!
* Narrow Predictor Bottleneck (D = 192, 4 Transformer layers):
  - Predictor receives context representations + learnable target position queries to predict target latents Y_hat.
  - Narrow capacity forces high-level semantic representation learning into the ViT encoder.
* Online Modality Dropout (p_drop = 0.25):
  - Randomly zeroes out MRI channels during training, instilling intrinsic robustness to missing sequences.

SPEAKER SCRIPT:
"Unlike Masked Autoencoders that reconstruct raw voxels and waste capacity modeling acquisition noise, JEPA predicts
representations directly in latent space.
We crop background air and resample to an isotropic 128-cubed grid. An 8-cubed 3D patch embedding yields 4,096 tokens.
Random patch masking fails on 3D MRI because dense spatial autocorrelation allows trivial interpolation. We instead
sample 6 anisotropic target cuboids across axial, coronal, and sagittal planes, enforcing a 25% brain-tissue threshold.
For the context, we use 3D Breadth-First Search to build a contiguous 37.5% volume cluster. By gathering only the 1,536
visible context tokens into self-attention, we eliminate mask tokens and reduce attention FLOPs by 85.9%."


SLIDE 5: VISReg: Heuristic-Free Anti-Collapse Mathematics
TITLE:
Mathematical Non-Collapse: Sliced-Wasserstein VISReg & Rank Verification

CORE MESSAGE:
Statistical regularization on an auxiliary projector eliminates momentum teachers, mathematically preventing
dimensional collapse and increasing effective spectral rank by +123% over from-scratch transformers.

DEEP DIVE:
* The Dimensional Collapse Threat:
  - In a single-encoder architecture without an EMA teacher, minimizing prediction loss L_JEPA = Smooth_L1(Y_hat, Y_tgt)
    has a trivial global minimum: mapping all inputs to a static, collapsed constant representation.
* VISReg Decoupled Moments Formulation:
  L_VISReg = lambda_c * (||mu||^2 / D) + lambda_sc * (sum_d (1 - sigma_d)^2 / D) + lambda_sh * W_2^2(P_theta, N(0, I))
  1. Center Regularization (lambda_c = 1.0): Anchors mean token embeddings to the origin.
  2. Scale Regularization (lambda_sc = 1.0): Penalizes variance deviations from unity across all D dimensions.
  3. Shape Regularization (lambda_sh = 4.0): Minimizes Sliced-Wasserstein distance to an isotropic Gaussian over
     M = 1024 1D random projection rays:
     W_2^2 = (1/M) * sum_m ||sort(u_m^T z) - sort(u_m^T v)||^2, where v ~ N(0, I).
* The Auxiliary Projector Role (384 -> 1024 -> 128):
  - Absorbs the strict isotropic hypersphere constraint, preventing over-regularization of the ViT backbone (D = 384)
    and allowing the encoder to preserve structured, anisotropic covariance for downstream multi-compartment segmentation.
* Empirical Proof of Non-Collapse (Effective Spectral Rank S^2):
  - Evaluated on covariance eigenvalues lambda_k across tissue tokens: S^2 = (sum lambda_k)^2 / sum(lambda_k^2).
  - Scratch UNETR collapses to S^2 = 30.95 (only 8.1% of D = 384 capacity utilized).
  - VisReg UNETR maintains S^2 = 69.13 (18.0% utilization) ===> +123% increase in rank utilization!
  - VisReg FPN maintains S^2 = 81.78 (21.3% utilization).

SPEAKER SCRIPT:
"Without an EMA teacher, how do we mathematically prevent the single encoder from collapsing? We implement VISReg:
Variance-Invariance-Shape Regularization. We constrain the first two moments by centering the mean and forcing unit
variance across all dimensions. For higher-order moments, we compute the Sliced-Wasserstein distance against an
isotropic Gaussian across 1,024 random projection rays.
Crucially, this regularization is applied to an auxiliary 2-layer projector, which absorbs the isotropic constraint
and allows the ViT encoder to maintain rich, anisotropic covariance.
We mathematically prove non-collapse by monitoring Effective Spectral Rank on covariance eigenvalues: training from
scratch collapses to an effective rank of 30.95, whereas our VISReg backbone maintains a rank of 69.13 to 81.78—a
123% increase in dimensionality utilization."


SLIDE 6: Evaluation Protocol & Probing Methodology
TITLE:
Evaluation Protocol: Volumetric Metrics & Dual Probing Regimes

CORE MESSAGE:
We evaluate overlap accuracy and boundary distances across three clinically structured stress tiers,
contrasting pure frozen latent decodability against end-to-end fine-tuning.

DEEP DIVE:
* Volumetric Clinical Metrics:
  1. 3D Dice Similarity Coefficient (Dice %): Measures volumetric spatial overlap across WT, TC, and ET.
  2. 95th Percentile Hausdorff Distance (HD95, in voxels): Robust maximum boundary surface distance,
     filtering out extreme outlier noise (empty mask penalty assigned bounding box diagonal: 128*sqrt(3) ≈ 221.7 voxels).
  3. Macro-Average Dice: Unweighted mean across the three clinical sub-compartments: Mean = (WT + TC + ET) / 3.
* Dual Downstream Probing Regimes:
  - Frozen Backbone Probing (freeze_encoder = True): Locks all pre-trained ViT parameters, training only decoder
    weights to rigorously measure the intrinsic spatial decodability of self-supervised representations.
  - End-to-End Fine-Tuning: Adapts all backbone and decoder weights to evaluate maximum downstream performance.
* Three Clinical Benchmark Tiers:
  1. In-Distribution Canonical Benchmark: 60-epoch full-data training evaluated on held-out test volumes (N = 242).
  2. Annotation Efficiency Scaling: 25-epoch training across 6 strictly nested annotation tiers (1% to 100%).
  3. Out-of-Distribution Stress Testing:
     - Synthetic Scanner Shifts: Additive 3D Rician noise (sigma = 0.08) and quadratic B1 RF field bias.
     - Emergency Clinical Triage: Complete 4-sequence acquisition vs. FLAIR-only and T1c-only acquisitions.

SPEAKER SCRIPT:
"To evaluate our models, we measure volumetric 3D Dice scores and 95th-percentile Hausdorff boundary distances across
Whole Tumor, Tumor Core, and Enhancing Tumor, following the official BraTS protocol.
We evaluate two probing regimes: full fine-tuning, and frozen probing where all ViT backbone weights are locked to
measure the intrinsic quality of pre-trained latents without retraining.
We assess performance across three distinct tiers: first, the canonical in-distribution benchmark on our 242 held-out
test volumes; second, label efficiency across budgets from 1% to 100%; and third, out-of-distribution stress testing
under continuous scanner shifts and emergency missing-sequence triage."


SLIDE 7: Baseline Model Specifications & Parameter Calibration
TITLE:
Baseline Architectures & Strict Parameter Calibration

CORE MESSAGE:
Scientific fairness is enforced by calibrating all deployed models within a strict 19M to 25M parameter envelope;
pre-training scaffolding is completely discarded prior to deployment.

DETAILED ARCHITECTURAL PROFILES:
* 1. 3D Residual UNet (BraTS3DUNet, 19.23M Parameters):
  - 5 Encoder/Decoder Stages: [32, 64, 128, 256, 512] channels.
  - 2 convolutions per residual block with Instance Normalization and LeakyReLU.
  - Stride-2 downsampling; 3 multi-scale Deep Supervision heads (DS1 at 16^3, DS2 at 32^3, DS3 at 64^3).
* 2. 3D nnU-Net Baseline (BraTS3DnnUNet / DynUNet, 22.75M Parameters):
  - MONAI self-configuring biomedical standard topology: [32, 64, 128, 256, 320] channels across 5 stages.
  - 3x3x3 convolutions with deep supervision branches at stages 16^3, 32^3, and 64^3.
* 3. Pre-Training JEPA (SingleEncoderJEPA3D, 19.80M Parameters):
  - ViT Backbone (16.56M): 8 Transformer blocks, D = 384, 6 heads, MLP expansion 4x.
  - Narrow Predictor (2.71M): 4 Transformer blocks, D = 192, 4 heads.
  - Auxiliary Projector (0.53M): 2-layer MLP (384 -> 1024 -> 128) with GroupNorm.
  - Scaffolding Discard: Predictor (2.71M) and Projector (0.53M) are discarded after pre-training!
* 4. ViT + Multi-Scale FPN Decoder (24.82M Parameters):
  - Pure Latent Probe: ViT Backbone (16.56M) + FPN Decoder (8.27M). Zero raw-image skip connections.
  - Lateral taps extracted from ViT layers L2, L4, L6, L8 (projections 192 -> 96 -> 48 -> 24 ch).
* 5. ViT + Hybrid UNETR Decoder (24.91M Parameters):
  - Dual-Stream Probe: ViT Backbone (16.56M) + Conv Stem (0.09M) + Decoder (8.27M).
  - Shallow convolutional stem (stem_128 @ 16ch, stem_64 @ 32ch) provides high-frequency boundary cues.

SPEAKER SCRIPT:
"A vital prerequisite for scientific credibility is parameter fairness. All models operate within the same 19M to
25M parameter envelope. Our pre-training model is 19.8M parameters, but after pre-training, the 3.2M-parameter predictor
and projector scaffolding are discarded.
The final deployed segmentation model is 24.8M parameters for FPN and 24.9M for UNETR—directly comparable to 3D nnU-Net
at 22.8M and 3D Residual UNet at 19.2M.
Furthermore, we contrast two decoder philosophies: FPN is a pure latent probe with zero access to raw image pixels,
while UNETR is a dual-stream architecture fusing ViT latents with a shallow convolutional stem."

SLIDE 12: Conclusions, Clinical Implications & Future Directions
TITLE:
Thesis Conclusions, Practical Translation & Future Work

CORE MESSAGE:
Single-encoder 3D VISReg JEPA eliminates heuristic momentum teachers, prevents dimensional collapse, and delivers decisive real-world advantages where clinical labels and sequences are scarce.

1. SCIENTIFIC & METHODOLOGICAL CONCLUSIONS:
   * Heuristic-Free Anti-Collapse (VISReg): Sliced-Wasserstein statistical regularization on an auxiliary projector
     eliminates the need for empirical EMA teachers, saving ~40% memory and doubling effective rank (+123%, S^2 >= 69.1).
   * Competitive Full-Data Accuracy: Fine-tuned VisReg UNETR achieves 69.66% mean Dice (84.11% WT), within 1.2pp
     of deeply supervised 3D nnU-Net (70.86%) and beating 3D Residual UNet (65.83%, +3.83pp).
   * Frozen Probing Power: Completely frozen ViT latents achieve 69.34% mean Dice (only -0.32pp below fine-tuning!),
     proving that 3D JEPA features capture rich spatial geometry without downstream architectural retraining.

2. CLINICAL & PRACTICAL TRANSLATION:
   * Institutional Adaptation with Small Cohorts: When adapting glioma segmentation to a new hospital with limited
     expert annotations (<= 58 volumes / 5% data), VisReg UNETR outperforms 3D nnU-Net by +7.39pp (54.45% vs. 47.06%).
   * Robust Emergency Triage Protocols: VisReg JEPA provides a proven clinical safety net when emergency acquisitions
     are degraded or interrupted (leading FLAIR-only by +4.82pp, and shielding T1c-only by +12.67pp).

3. LIMITATIONS & BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:
   * Computational Overhead of 3D ViT: Full 3D self-attention across 16^3 tokens remains compute-intensive during pre-training.
   * Multi-Modal Co-Registration: The pipeline assumes standard 1mm^3 isotropic co-registration across MRI contrasts.

4. FUTURE RESEARCH DIRECTIONS:
   * Multi-Pathology SSL: Extending 3D VISReg pre-training beyond gliomas to multi-institution stroke, multiple sclerosis,
     and metastatic brain lesion datasets.
   * Sparse & Multi-Scale Attention: Incorporating windowed or flash 3D attention to enable native sub-millimeter (0.5mm^3)
     volumetric pre-training.
